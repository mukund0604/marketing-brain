# Marketing Brain

**What it is:** a 360° marketing memory for one brand, stored in HydraDB, with an agent that answers questions and generates on-brand creatives from it, shows exactly which memories it used, and learns from client feedback.

**Who uses it (assumption, inferred from the build brief):** agency account managers, strategists and creative leads at Zocket working on a client brand; at the hackathon, judges watching a 2–3 minute screen-recorded demo.

**Primary tasks:** ask about campaign performance, consumer sentiment, competitors and brand rules; brief and compare creatives; relay client feedback; promote recurring corrections into skills; inspect what the brain holds.

**Proof the product must make visible:** the retrieval trace (queries, ranked scores, used vs ignored, graph paths) and the with-brain vs no-memory comparison.

**Brand commitments:**
- Visual standard: category canon, executed at full fidelity, sitting alongside Glean, Linear and Vercel dashboards. Light theme, left-sidebar app shell (user choice, 2026-10-12).
- Demo brand Tidelight and its competitors are fictional; all data is synthetic and must stay labeled as such.

**Mode:** Operate.
